# Product Requirements Document: PNJ Wedding Campaign 2025

## Introduction/Overview

This PRD outlines the requirements for PNJ's Integrated Marketing Communication (IMC) campaign for the 2025 wedding season (August-October). The campaign aims to address the growing marriage hesitation trend among young Vietnamese while strengthening PNJ's position as a leading wedding jewelry brand.

**Problem Statement:** Young Vietnamese couples are increasingly hesitant about marriage due to economic uncertainty, shifting values, and lifestyle priorities. PNJ needs to break through this barrier and strengthen its emotional connection and cultural relevance in the wedding jewelry segment.

**Goal:** Create an IMC campaign that increases market share by 5% and revenue by 35% during the peak wedding season while building authentic emotional connections with target audiences.

## Goals

1. **Business Goals:**
   - Increase 5% market share in wedding jewelry segment vs 2024
   - Increase 35% revenue during peak wedding season (Aug-Oct 2025)
   - Accelerate penetration in tier 2/3 markets while maintaining tier 1

2. **Marketing Goals:**
   - Strengthen brand image as wedding jewelry brand celebrating authentic love
   - Increase brand love through emotionally resonant storytelling
   - Trigger purchase intent through engaging online/offline experiences

3. **Communication Goals:**
   - Build emotional connection with target audience
   - Address marriage hesitation with positive, culturally relevant messaging
   - Establish PNJ as cultural authority in wedding jewelry

## User Stories

**Primary Target - "THE LOVERS" (26-35 years old):**
- As a young professional in a serious relationship, I want to feel confident about marriage despite economic uncertainty, so that I can plan my future with my partner
- As someone preparing for marriage, I want to find jewelry that reflects my cultural values and personal story, so that my wedding feels authentic and meaningful
- As a modern Vietnamese couple, I want to celebrate our love in a way that honors tradition while embracing contemporary values, so that our wedding feels both timeless and relevant

**Secondary Target - "MOTHER OF THE BRIDE/GROOM" (36-60 years old):**
- As a parent helping plan a wedding, I want to ensure my child gets quality jewelry that's worth the investment, so that I feel confident about the purchase decision
- As someone with financial planning experience, I want to see clear value in wedding jewelry purchases, so that I can justify the expense to myself and family

## Functional Requirements

### Campaign Platform & Strategy
1. The system must develop a hybrid communication platform that evolves "Có Nhau" into a broader, more inclusive concept
2. The system must integrate 2025 social sentiment and cultural elements as guided by the creative concept
3. The system must create a phased campaign approach with different focuses per month (Aug-Oct)
4. The system must balance traditional and digital media channels with 10B VNĐ budget

### Product Strategy
5. The system must introduce a brand-new wedding jewelry collection with unique storytelling
6. The system must position the new collection to address cultural pride and modern love values
7. The system must ensure the collection appeals to both primary and secondary target audiences
8. The system must create product storytelling that enhances appeal through symbolism and design

### Target Audience Focus
9. The system must prioritize "THE LOVERS" (26-35 years old) as primary target audience
10. The system must address marriage hesitation through positive, empowering messaging
11. The system must create content that resonates with tier 1, 2, and 3 markets
12. The system must build emotional connection that drives purchase intent

### Media & Channel Strategy
13. The system must implement balanced traditional + digital media mix
14. The system must create engaging online and offline experiences
15. The system must capture attention of those in consideration stage
16. The system must drive conversion through integrated touchpoints

### Measurement & Success
17. The system must track market share increase (5% target)
18. The system must measure revenue growth (35% target during peak season)
19. The system must monitor brand love and emotional connection metrics
20. The system must evaluate cultural relevance and social impact

## Non-Goals (Out of Scope)

- **Relaunching existing collections:** Focus is on introducing new collection, not refreshing existing ones
- **Heavy nationalistic messaging:** Social sentiment integration should be guided by creative concept, not forced
- **Digital-only approach:** Must include traditional media channels
- **Luxury positioning focus:** Campaign should be accessible while maintaining premium quality
- **Regional-only focus:** Must maintain tier 1 market while expanding to tier 2/3
- **Single-phase campaign:** Must implement phased approach with different monthly focuses

## Design Considerations

- **Cultural Sensitivity:** All creative elements must respect Vietnamese cultural values and traditions
- **Emotional Resonance:** Visual and messaging design should evoke authentic emotional connections
- **Premium Aesthetic:** Design should reflect PNJ's sophisticated brand positioning
- **Multi-channel Consistency:** Creative elements must work across traditional and digital channels
- **Accessibility:** Design should be inclusive and accessible to diverse audience segments

## Technical Considerations

- **Budget Management:** 10B VNĐ must be allocated efficiently across channels and phases
- **Measurement Framework:** Must establish KPIs and tracking mechanisms for all objectives
- **Creative Asset Management:** Must create assets that work across multiple channels and formats
- **Timeline Coordination:** Phased approach requires careful coordination of different campaign elements
- **Stakeholder Alignment:** Must align with PNJ's existing brand guidelines and business objectives

## Success Metrics

### Primary Metrics
- **Market Share:** 5% increase in wedding jewelry segment vs 2024
- **Revenue:** 35% increase during peak wedding season (Aug-Oct)
- **Brand Awareness:** Measurable increase in wedding jewelry category consideration

### Secondary Metrics
- **Brand Love:** Positive sentiment and emotional connection scores
- **Cultural Relevance:** Social media engagement and cultural conversation participation
- **Purchase Intent:** Conversion rates from consideration to purchase
- **Regional Expansion:** Increased penetration in tier 2/3 markets

### Measurement Timeline
- **Weekly:** Social media engagement, content performance
- **Monthly:** Brand sentiment, market share indicators
- **Post-Campaign:** Full ROI analysis, market share final numbers
